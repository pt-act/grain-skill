Questions

Do you take reservations?
Only by phone, only for six or more.

Can I bring a bottle?
Yes — 15€ corkage, and we will serve it properly.

Is the list fixed?
No. The chalkboard is the list. What is written is what is open.

Do you sell bottles to take away?
Most of the list, at retail, with a small discount on six.

Come and sit down

Twelve seats, one list, no reservations for fewer than six. Walk in, find a stool, point at the chalkboard.
