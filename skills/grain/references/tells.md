# Tells

The dated registry of generated-page tells — the devices that mark a page as assembled rather than decided. Each entry: the tell, why it reads as generic, and what replaces it. Check this list when a page feels wrong but nothing in the gate fired; a tell is a failure of category, not of rule.

## The layout tells

| Tell | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Centred headline over a subhead over three feature cards | The average of every landing page ever generated | Left-aligned type, one claim per section, sections with different compositions |
| A hero that is a full-bleed stock photo with centred white text | The placeholder for "premium" | The dealt hero: a composition, not a cover |
| Cards in a row, identical, with icons on top | Furniture, not structure | The dealt blueprints: hangs, ledgers, sequences, margins |
| A grid of six logos | Proof by accumulation | One honest claim with its proof |
| A testimonial carousel | Content deferred to a control | One real voice, set as a statement, if a real one exists |

## The styling tells

| Tell | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A soft gradient on the ground | The generator's favourite sky | Flat colour, or a real photograph |
| Shadows under every card | Depth faked for separation | A tone step between surfaces |
| A pill button with a shadow | The SaaS default | Flat fill, square or fully round, decided once |
| Rounded "soft" corners (9–16px) | Nothing chose them | Square or fully round |
| Hairline rules between sections | Separation without decisions | Space, a tone step, a change of scale |
| A backdrop-blur navigation bar | Floating chrome | A plain bar on the ground |

## The type tells

| Tell | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny uppercase letter-spaced labels everywhere | The costume of technical seriousness | Sentence case at a readable size |
| Monospace captions and labels | Terminal cosplay | The text face; mono only in real code |
| Index numbers (01 / 02 / 03) | Order performed, not present | Position, size, or words |
| Five or more font sizes | No hierarchy, so all sizes | Three sizes, nothing in between |
| Centred everything | A pamphlet, not a page | Left-aligned by default; one centred moment at most |

## The copy tells

| Tell | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Welcome to [name]" | Filler where a claim belongs | The subject and its one thing, in the headline |
| Headlines that describe the section ("Our Services") | A table of contents | A claim ("Three rooms, each facing the courtyard") |
| Adjectives with no nouns ("artisanal, curated, handcrafted") | Decoration | The thing, its material, its price |
| "Coming soon" | The page admitting it is not built | Real content, or the section cut |

## The imagery tells

| Tell | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A row of small stock photos | Decoration at thumbnail scale | One dominant image, used as a place |
| Photos with white borders and shadows on the ground | Scrapbook cosplay | Same crops, square corners, no frames |
| Abstract shapes behind the hero | "Modern" with no subject | The subject's own material, drawn or photographed |
| Fake dashboards of grey bars | A product no one has seen | A real interface fragment (`data-ui`) or none |

## Dated entries

Retire a tell when it stops reading as costume and starts reading as craft. As of this writing: the AI-slop gradient (2023–), the monospace-caption revival (2024–), the "Bento grid" of rounded boxes (2023–2025). Check the list before adding a new device — it may already be here.
